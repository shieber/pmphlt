After a few years' break from blogging, I'm back.

My blogs "The Occasional Pamphlet" and "Ground Truth" were formerly hosted on Harvard's blogging platform, which was mothballed in 2023. I've now resurrected them, combining them (as described [here](/about)) and transferring them to this platform. (Thanks, GitHub Pages!) 

I'm planning to return to my occasional pamphleteering, prompted by my work on [grade inflation at Harvard College and its negative knock-on effects](https://oue.fas.harvard.edu/files/2026/02/Grading-policy-proposal-260205.pdf). More on that in future posts.

Of course, I expect no one is watching this space anymore, it having been idle for so long, so please subscribe via [RSS](/atom.xml) or [email](https://follow.it/the-occasional-pamphlet) and tell your friends.
